FPSL Strategy Backtest

10-Year Simulation | $10M Initial Capital | 10% Max Position

The Strategy: Identify biotechnology companies with upcoming Phase 2 or Phase 3 clinical trial readouts. The model uses an AI to predict successful clinical outcomes with 100% accuracy. The fund enters a position 84 calendar days (approx. 60 trading days) prior to the scheduled catalyst date. During this holding period, the highly shorted shares are lent out to short-sellers via Fully Paid Securities Lending (FPSL) to collect massive borrow fees (40-120% APR). The position is exited 2 days after the catalyst date to capture the gap-up return.
